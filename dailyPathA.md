Here is the raw, transactional open-source playbook. No buzzwords, just the
actual mechanics of how to use free work to get paid.

The "Get Paid" Open Source Path:

Step 1: Target the Money, Not the Passion. Do not contribute to a solo dev’s
weekend project. Find a tool built by a funded startup (Series A/B) or a
corporate entity. They have actual payroll budgets but are usually drowning in
backlog issues.

Step 2: Grind the Garbage (Months 1-2). Do not try to build a massive new
feature. You will just annoy the core team. Instead, do the work the paid devs
hate:

  - Fix broken links in the documentation.
  - Write unit tests for edge cases.
  - Triage the issue tracker and close stale tickets. Make yourself an
    undeniable utility to the core team.

Step 3: The Pivot (Month 3). Once your name is recognizable and you have 10+
merged PRs, stop being quiet. DM the core maintainer on X or Discord. Because
you just saved them 20 hours of annoying work over the last two months, they
will answer. Ask if they have overflow contract work or if they can intro you to
a company using their tool.
